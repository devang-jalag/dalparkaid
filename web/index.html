Verified Campus & Municipal Context

Why We Are Validating DalParkAid

Our Campus2Market Validate customer discovery focuses on verified structural bottlenecks in university and downtown Halifax commuter parking:

1. Permits Do Not Guarantee a Parking Stall

Source: dal.ca

Dalhousie uses HotSpot virtual license-plate permits and explicitly notes that purchasing a General Permit does not guarantee an available space, as permits are oversold to maximize surface lot utilization on a first-come, first-served basis.

2. Complex Daytime, Evening & Overnight Rules

Source: dal.ca

Daytime campus parking rules run from 6:00 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays. Evening rules apply from 4:30 PM to 1:30 AM, while general overnight parking is prohibited between 1:30 AM and 6:00 AM under penalty of ticketing or towing.

3. High Urban Search Time & Municipal Fines

Sources: halifax.ca / INRIX

Urban mobility research (INRIX) shows drivers spend 17 to 40+ hours per year searching for parking. When campus lots fill up and commuters spill onto city streets, HRM parking violations carry a $45 fine ($40 within 7 days).

Help & Quick Guide

Dalhousie Parking Rules & Simulator FAQ

How do Dalhousie University parking permits work? ▼

Dalhousie uses virtual permits managed through HotSpot, tied directly to your vehicle license plate. Commuters can hold General Permits, Reserved Permits, or pay for Short-Term hourly parking via the HotSpot app. Because general permits are oversold relative to physical spaces, stalls are filled first-come, first-served.

What happens after 4:30 PM on campus lots? ▼

Standard daytime restrictions run from 6:00 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays. During evening hours (4:30 PM to 1:30 AM) and weekends, many restricted lots open up for broader permit access, and certain locations (such as the McCain Building parkade) offer flat-rate evening short-term parking. Overnight parking between 1:30 AM and 6:00 AM is prohibited without a special overnight pass.

Are the numbers on this website live sensor feeds or a simulation? ▼

The lot capacities (1,495 stalls across 28 Dalhousie lots) and GPS coordinates are real campus data, and the Halifax weather feed is fetched live from Open-Meteo. The availability scores, animated vehicles, and commuter calculator on this web page run in an interactive sandbox so visitors and Campus2Market reviewers can test different times of day, weather conditions, and check-ins.
